# Lineage
Sync v1 (2020) had per-operation endpoints and a local change log. Sync v3 (#899, Nov 2021) brought base, local, and remote with a three-way merge and one batch upsert; `docs/sync.md` describes it. Sharing and the staged, validated, lazy tree landed together in Aug 2022 (#1221, #1254), and the tree refactors with the merge re-implementation followed that winter (#1441–#1454). db-rs replaced hmdb in Feb 2023 and made transactions atomic. Sync 3.2 (#2206, Oct 2023) stopped holding the global lock through network calls. `safe_write` and the word-level merge (#2779, Oct 2024) made collaboration lockless. Versioned records (#3599, #3737, 2025) prepared for document history (#190). Shared-with-me (#3999, #3990, Dec 2025) made pending shares a browsable, editable pane — core had allowed reading and writing them since 2022; lazy files (#4240, Mar 2026) stopped fetching everything; universal links (#4289, #4442, Apr 2026) put the external URL on https; db-rs 0.4 arrived in Sep 2026 (#5078).

`docs/sync.md` remains the best account of the original constraints and of repairing invariants rather than preventing violations; its routines, versions, and server storage sections describe a system that no longer exists. The 2022 sharing UX RFC is at `docs/design-ux/sharing.md@e4bbff2da`.
