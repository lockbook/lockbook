# Writing in Markdown
Lockbook notes are written in Markdown: plain text with a little punctuation for formatting. Many other apps read it too, so your notes aren't tied to Lockbook.

The punctuation tends to look like the style it makes, and the toolbar can add it for you.

## Styles
Most Markdown styles work by surrounding text in one or two matching punctuations:
* one asterisk for *italic*: `*italic*`
* two asterisks for **bold**: `**bold**`
* two equals for ==highlight==: `==highlight==`
* two underscores for __underline__: `__underline__`
* two tildes for ~~strike-through~~: `~~strike-through~~`

## Structure
Add structure to your document with punctuation made to look like what it does.

**Headings**
```md
# Heading
## Subheading
```

**Lists**
```md
* one item
* another item
```

**Numbered Lists**
```md
1. First item
2. Second item
```

## The Toolbar
Select a word or paragraph and tap a toolbar icon to add the punctuation for you. The [formatting reference](https://lockbook.net/docs/formatting-reference.html) lists everything else Lockbook supports.
