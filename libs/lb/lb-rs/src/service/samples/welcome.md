# Welcome to Lockbook

Lockbook is a private place for your notes, plans, and files. Everything you keep here is end-to-end encrypted — only you can read it.

Not sure where to start? We made you a `samples` folder:

- [A todo list](samples/todo.md) — re-order and check things off as you go. From groceries to launch plans
- [A journal](samples/journal.md) — a quiet place for daily reflections
- [The bitcoin whitepaper](samples/bitcoin.pdf) — store and read PDFs
- [A drawing](samples/drawing.svg) — sketch your ideas with the built-in canvas

Edit them, rename them, or delete them — they're yours.
