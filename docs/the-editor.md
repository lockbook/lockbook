# Writing in Markdown
Lockbook writes notes in Markdown, the universal standard for formatted text. It’s supported by hundreds of [existing apps](file-management.md#ingress--egress) for good reasons.

Markdown structures your notes by adding simple punctuation that looks and feels a lot like the styles it produces. New to Markdown? Don’t worry: you’ll learn your usual text styles in minutes and let our toolbar handle the rest.

## Styles
Most Markdown styles work by surrounding text in one or two matching punctuations:
* one asterisk for `*italic*`
* two asterisks for `**bold**`
* two equals for `==highlight==`
* two underscores for `__underline__`
* two tildes for `~~strike-through~~`

## Structure
Add structure to your document with punctuation made to look like what it does.

**Headings**
```md
# Heading
## Subheading
```

**Lists**
```md
* one item
* another item
```

**Numbered Lists**
```md
1. First item
2. Second item
```

## The Toolbar
These are just a few examples of what Lockbook can do. The [formatting reference](editor.md) has the rest. To skip the punctuation, try selecting a word or paragraph and tapping an icon on the toolbar, which adds the punctuation for you. It’s a fast way to learn your favorites or skip the punctuation on your phone.
