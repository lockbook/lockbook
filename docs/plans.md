# Plans
Our free tier comes with everything you need to find out if Lockbook is for you, including:
* Unlimited synced devices
* Unlimited [shared files](sharing.md)
* 25 MB compressed storage (~100 MB in practice)

In practice, our free tier is enough for virtually unlimited plain text notes plus a handful of drawings, images, and PDFs. For basics like grocery or todo lists, it’s enough forever. Richer content or more attachments is what makes premium attractive.

Our premium tier is **$2.99 per month** and adds **30 GB of storage**. You can sign up in the app on any platform, with billing handled through the App Store on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, Google Play on Android, or Stripe on Windows and Linux.

## Cancelling
Cancel through the store you paid with: your [App Store](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) or [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481) subscriptions, or settings in the desktop app for Stripe. A store subscription lasts until the end of the period you paid for. On Windows and Linux, cancelling ends premium right away, and you’ll need to get under the free limit first. If you’re over the free limit when premium ends, your files stay safe and readable, but changes that add to your usage won’t sync until you make room or upgrade again. If a purchase doesn’t show up, [get in touch](https://discord.gg/lockbook).
