# Welcome
Welcome to the official documentation site for [Lockbook](https://lockbook.net). This is where we publish guides on how to use our product.

Lockbook is a private & collaborative notes app that syncs notes across your devices, your friends’, and nobody else’s. It’s built on secure cloud storage that never [sees your files](how-lockbook-protects-you.md) because they’re encrypted before they leave your device and you hold the keys. Your Lockbook is written and organized in universal formats that work with hundreds of [existing apps](file-management.md#ingress--egress) so you can share your work anywhere and take it with you if you leave, and it costs nothing to get started with our full feature set. What are you waiting for?
