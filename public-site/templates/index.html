Markdown

Formatting that resolves as you type.

Encryption

We can’t read your notes. That’s structural, not a policy.

Your keys are generated on your device and never leave it. Files are encrypted before they sync. Sharing hands a key to a person you name — never to us. We don’t ask for your email, your phone number, or a password.
